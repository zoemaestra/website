DIY Electrolysis - Usage

This part is harder, because it requires some trial and error and real world experience. I recommend just trying to slide the needle into the follicle with the device off to get a feel for it first and when you do actually run it live, do some test runs some place you don't mind getting scarred so much.

It's not a super pleasant sensation to feel the needle go in and hit the end of the follicle, nor is it pleasant to actually zap (I'm sure some of you will disagree though :p ).

Hair type Amount of lye (LU)
Fine, unpigmented vellus hair 10 - 15
Fine, pigmented, soft hair 10 - 20
Medium/shallow terminal hair 15 - 30
Deep terminal hair 30 - 45
Very deep terminal hair 45 - 60

Galvanic electrolysis does not kill hairs by zapping them, it does so by generating lye in the follicle which chemically kills it off.

The above table is a rough guide for how much lye to use for different hair types. The amount of lye is measured in Lye Units (LU). You can set how much lye you want to aim to generate in the follicle in the device's settings.

Once you've set your desired settings, insert the needle into the follicle and hold down the foot pedal until it automatically stops generating lye.

It is important to sanitise the site you're working on with 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes beforehand for hygiene and safety. 70% IPA and not 99%, and you're sanitising not sterilising. The difference is fairly irrelevant to the guide, it just bugs me when people mix the two up.

The needles are consumables. They are not meant to be used more than once per session. However, I don't see much reason you couldn't re-sterilise them by putting them in sealing sterile pouches and autoclaving them if they're still physically good. Don't do this unless you are experienced and have the proper equipment to do so.

I hope this guide has helped you and encouraged you to pursue DIY electrolysis hair removal!
